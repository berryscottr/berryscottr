Disclosures

Important information

Portfolio demo. This site presents a personal research project as an engineering and data-science case study. It is not an offer, a fund, or a service, and no money is managed or solicited.

Hypothetical performance

All performance shown is hypothetical. It comes from a computer simulation of the strategy on historical minute-bar and daily price data from Jan 3, 2017 to Nov 5, 2026. It does not reflect actual trading. No account has traded this strategy, and no client or investor has earned these returns.

Simulated results have inherent limits. They are prepared with the benefit of hindsight, can't account for every market factor (liquidity, market impact, borrow availability, order-execution details, outages), and may not reflect how the strategy would perform with real capital. The research process was designed to limit these effects (a three-way time split, a ledger of every configuration tried, a one-shot holdout read against pre-registered bands), but it cannot remove them.

Assumptions

  • Costs: 1–10 basis points of slippage per fill plus a half-tick floor, SEC and FINRA fees, a 1% annual short-borrow fee, and 5% annual interest on borrowed cash. No commissions.
  • Fills occur on the minute bar after each decision. Quantities are whole shares.
  • Idle cash earns the return of a short-term T-bill ETF (BIL, later SGOV).
  • Returns are before tax. All gains would be short-term.
  • Results are for an account of about $50,000. Capacity at larger sizes hasn't been studied.
  • Projections resample the strategy's own simulated history. They are not forecasts and don't predict future results.

Benchmarks

Benchmarks are exchange-traded funds (for example SPY for the S&P 500), using split- and dividend-adjusted prices. The hedge-fund comparisons are ETFs that follow or replicate hedge-fund styles, not hedge funds or hedge-fund indices. Benchmarks have different risks, liquidity and fees from this strategy.

No offer or advice

Nothing on this site is an offer to sell, or a solicitation of an offer to buy, any security, fund, account or service, and nothing here is investment, legal or tax advice. Past performance, actual or simulated, does not guarantee future results. Every investment can lose money.

Data and names

Market data: Alpaca Markets (SIP minute and daily bars) and SEC EDGAR (company classification). Pairs Engine is a working name. The strategy isn't affiliated with, or endorsed by, any broker, exchange or data provider named here.
