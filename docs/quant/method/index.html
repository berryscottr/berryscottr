Method & integrity

A research process you can audit end to end

Anyone can show a beautiful backtest. The useful question is how it was produced: how many things were tried, what was held back, and what happened to the parts that didn't go our way.

From the whole market to a trade, every week

Universe

Every listed US operating company, including stocks that later delisted. Funds and ETFs excluded by SEC classification.

Discover

Weekly whole-market scan: a correlation pre-filter, then cointegration tests on a year of daily prices, with multiple-testing control.

Select

Rank the surviving pairs by how persistently they've qualified, with sector and per-stock concentration caps.

Trade

On minute bars, enter when a pair's spread stretches unusually far from its mean, exit when it reverts, with a loss stop and a maximum holding time.

Account

Whole shares, next-minute fills, slippage on every fill, regulatory and borrow fees, margin interest, T-bill yield on idle cash.

The exact parameter values and the weekly pair lists are proprietary and available under NDA.

The controls

ControlWhat it means
Three-way time splitTune 2017–22 · validate 2023 → Oct 2025 · holdout Nov 2025 → Nov 2026. Never shuffled.
Every trial counted4,699 configurations (5,556 evaluations across 100 sweep runs) in a ledger. The deflated (luck-adjusted) Sharpe uses that count.
Regions, not pointsA setting is chosen by its neighbourhood: interior points only, with at least 75% of neighbours and 60% of years profitable.
Pre-registrationEach of the 27 search grids is a committed file with its purpose, written before it ran. Version control proves the order.
Rationed validationValidation results stay hidden by default. 3 logged looks in total, never used to choose between settings.
One-shot holdoutEnforced in code and logged. The bands for reading it were committed before the run (commit 811bde3).
No look-aheadA decision at minute t uses only data up to t and fills at t+1. Pair selection and hedge ratios use only data up to the formation date. Tests perturb the future and check that nothing changes.
Conservative costs1–10 basis points of slippage per fill plus a half-tick floor, SEC and FINRA fees, 1% a year short borrow, a 5% margin loan.
A null testIn a synthetic market with zero real pairs, 944 passed every statistical filter. Passing the screen is never treated as evidence; only out-of-sample profit counts.
Engineering145 automated tests; statistics checked against reference implementations; results bit-identical regardless of how many processors run them.

What we found wrong, ourselves

  • A data-window bug made one configuration's result depend on its neighbours in the same run. Fixing it cut the headline from 1.33 to 1.07, and every stage was re-run.
  • A configuration-rebuild bug would have made the holdout test the wrong model. Caught before the holdout; the fix reproduces sweep results to the last digit.
  • Pre-registered checks that failed are reported as failures, including one missed by 0.004 Sharpe.
  • Every error found becomes a written-up pitfall with an automated guard: a test, a runtime check or a refusal to run.

What isn't modelled yet

Capacity

Results are for a ~$50k account. Market impact at institutional size is unknown.

Survivorship

Some delisted stocks are missing from the data, which flatters results.

Borrow and fills

Borrow is a flat 1% a year; fills are next-minute plus assumed slippage, to be calibrated live.

Concentration

2020 drove much of the backtest; five pairs drove the holdout year.

Edge decay

The tune → validation → holdout decline may continue.

Tax

All gains are short-term; tax isn't modelled.
