Even in the stress case, 1.0× ends ahead of T-bills; 2.0× doesn't. That is the leverage lesson below.

Leverage

It scales mechanically, but only pays when the edge beats financing

Above 1×, the extra positions are paid for with cash that would otherwise earn T-bills; above 2×, with a margin loan at 5%. In the backtest the edge beat that cost easily. In the holdout year it didn't, so the plan is to start at 1.0× and scale only on live evidence. No margin limit was breached at any level, even 6×.
